Where is your child?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Where is your child? is a reminder app designed to help parents remember where their child is throughout the day.

Information stored by the app

The app allows you to create profiles for one or more children. For each child, you may enter their name and manually record a selected location, such as Mom, Dad, Grandparents, Daycare, Home, or Other.

The app may also store information associated with each child's profile, including check-in history, reminder settings, a profile photo selected by you, and a background photo selected by you.

Profile photos and background photos are optional. You choose whether to provide them.

How your information is stored

The information described above is stored locally on your device. Where is your child? does not send this information to the developer or operate a server that stores your child's information.

The app does not use GPS location tracking. The locations shown in the app are locations that you manually select. The app does not track your child's physical location.

Photos

If you choose to add a profile photo or background photo, the selected photo is stored locally as part of the app's data on your device.

The app does not upload your selected photos to the developer, does not use them for facial recognition, and does not use them for advertising or analytics.

The app only accesses photos that you choose to add to the app.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device to remind you to record your child's location.

Notifications are generated and scheduled by the app on your device. The app does not use a remote notification service to track your child.

Third-party services

Where is your child? does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party services to collect or sell your personal information.

The app does not use third-party services to store your child's profile information, photos, check-in history, or manually selected locations.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

Because the app does not send your stored information to us, we do not have access to your child's name, profile photo, background photo, manually selected locations, check-in history, or reminder settings.

Deleting your information

You can delete information stored by the app through the app's available controls. You can also remove the app and its locally stored app data from your device by deleting the app from your device.

Children's information

Where is your child? is designed for parents and caregivers. The app does not require a child to create an account or provide information directly to the developer.

Information about a child that a parent or caregiver chooses to enter into the app remains stored locally on the parent's or caregiver's device.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of the app change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
