Where is your child?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Where is your child? is a reminder app designed to help parents remember where their child is throughout the day.

Information stored by the app

The app allows you to enter your child's name and manually record a selected location, such as Mom, Dad, Grandparents, Daycare, Home, or Other.

The app may also store a background photo selected by you and your check-in history.

How your information is stored

The information described above is stored locally on your device. Where is your child? does not send this information to the developer or operate a server that stores your child's information.

The app does not use GPS location tracking. The locations shown in the app are locations that you manually select.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device to remind you to record your child's location. The app does not use a remote notification service to track your child.

Third-party services

Where is your child? does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party services to collect or sell your personal information.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Because the app does not send your stored information to us, we do not have access to your child's name, manually selected locations, check-in history, or background photo.

Deleting your information

You can remove the app and its locally stored app data from your device by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of the app change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
